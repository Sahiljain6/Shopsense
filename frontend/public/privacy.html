Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Welcome to ShopSense ("we", "our", or "us"). ShopSense is an AI-powered shopping copilot designed for consumers in India to search, compare, and discover verified e-commerce deals across Indian retailers in Indian Rupees (₹).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you access our web application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide and enhance our AI shopping copilot features:

  • Account & Identity Data: When you register via email or sign in with Google OAuth, we collect your name, email address, profile identifier, and authentication tokens.
  • Shopping Queries & Chat History: Products, categories, budget preferences, and questions you ask our AI assistant to tailor real-time recommendations.
  • Cart & Wishlist Data: Products you save or add to your demo cart/wishlist.
  • Technical Data: IP address (for rate limiting and fraud prevention), browser type, and operating system.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Compliance

Google OAuth Scopes: ShopSense strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Requested Scopes: We only request basic identification scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Limited Use: Google account data is exclusively used to authenticate your session and display your profile name inside the application.
  • No Data Sharing / Selling: We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data or personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No Model Training on Private Identity: Your private Google account credentials are never used to train generalized machine learning models.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate application purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your session.
  • To synthesize personalized product comparisons, pricing analysis, and deals across verified Indian merchants.
  • To prevent abuse, bot flooding, and unauthorized SSRF requests.
  • To maintain your conversation history across multiple visits.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords with bcrypt, HTTP-only secure cookie storage, and automated database encryption hosted on secure cloud infrastructure.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may request deletion of your account and all associated chat logs, wishlist items, and session tokens at any time by contacting our privacy team at sahil.jain24@sakec.ac.in. Upon receipt, your personal records will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

6. Cookies and Tracking

ShopSense uses essential HTTP-only cookies and local storage tokens solely for authentication and CSRF protection. We do not use third-party tracking pixels or behavioral advertising cookies.
